Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you register, create a booking, or contact us, we collect:

  • Personal identification: Full name, email address, phone number
  • Business information: Company name, business address (if applicable)
  • Booking details: Newspaper preferences, ad type, publication dates, ad content/artwork
  • Payment information: Billing address and payment details (processed securely via PayHere — we do not store your card details)
  • Communications: Messages sent via our contact form or email

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search terms
  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)

This information is collected using cookies, Google Analytics, and Meta Pixel (Facebook Pixel) — see the Cookies section below.

Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

Trusted Service Providers

  • PayHere (payhere.lk): Our payment gateway processes your payment securely. They handle your card/financial details and are PCI DSS compliant.
  • Google (Firebase & Analytics): We use Google Firebase to securely store booking data and Google Analytics to understand website usage.
  • Meta (Facebook Pixel): Used for measuring the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.
  • Newspapers/Publishers: Your ad content and artwork are shared with the relevant newspaper for publication. Personal contact details are not shared.
  • Google Apps Script: Used to send transactional emails (booking confirmations, invoices).

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by Sri Lankan law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of AdSpot Media, our customers, or the public.

⚠️ We will never: Sell your personal data to marketing companies, share your information with unauthorized third parties, or use your data for purposes unrelated to your booking and our services.

Data Storage and Security

Your personal information is stored securely using Google Firebase — a cloud platform with industry-standard encryption and security measures. We implement appropriate technical and organizational controls to protect your data including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Secure access controls and authentication for our admin systems
  • Regular security reviews of our data handling practices
  • Limited staff access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

While we take every reasonable precaution to protect your data, no internet transmission is completely secure. By using our services, you accept this inherent risk.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to:

  • Maintain your account and booking history
  • Comply with legal and tax obligations (typically 5–7 years)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

If you wish to have your data deleted, please contact us at adspot77@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days, subject to any legal obligations we must fulfil.
