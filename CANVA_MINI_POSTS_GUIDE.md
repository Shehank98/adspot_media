## 📋 GENERAL POSTING TIPS:

1. **Image Quality**: Make sure screenshots are high resolution (at least 1080px width)
2. **Aspect Ratio**: Use 1:1 (square) for Instagram, 4:5 for better Instagram feed visibility
3. **Branding**: Add your logo/brand colors in Canva
4. **Call-to-Action**: Always include website URL: **adspotmedia.lk**
5. **Hashtags** (add below the post):
   ```
   #AdSpotMedia #NewspaperAds #SriLankaAdvertising #ClassifiedAds
   #BoxAds #TenderNotices #LankaAds #SriLankaNews #BookYourAd
   ```
