FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking newspaper ads

For most newspapers, we can publish your ad within 24-48 hours if you book before the deadline (usually 6 PM the day before). For urgent requirements, we offer express service where your ad can appear in the next day's edition, subject to newspaper availability and deadline compliance.

You can cancel or modify your ad free of charge up to 24 hours before the publication date. If you cancel after this period but before the ad is sent to print, a 50% cancellation fee applies. Once the ad has been submitted to the newspaper, we cannot offer refunds, but we can help you reschedule to another date.

You can provide your own ready-made design, or we can help! For classified ads, simply provide the text and we'll format it according to newspaper standards. For display/box ads, we offer basic design assistance at no extra charge. If you need professional graphic design services, we can connect you with our design partners for an additional fee.

We provide proof of publication for every ad. After your ad is published, we'll send you a scanned copy or photo of the actual newspaper page showing your advertisement. For tender notices and legal ads, we can also provide certified copies from the newspaper office if required for official purposes.

We accept all major payment methods for your convenience: Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard), Online Bank Transfer, and Direct Bank Deposit. All online payments are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways. For corporate clients, we also offer invoice-based payment terms upon approval.
